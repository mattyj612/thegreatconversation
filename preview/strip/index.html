The Great Conversation

The Great Conversation is a yearly gathering in Toronto exploring the intellectual lineages that make technology possible.

Our first event took place May 27, 2026 at the Isabel Bader Theatre, where we convened 500 builders, writers, and artists.

Across a book fair, a lecture, and a panel, we argued that technology is best understood as a craft tradition, transmitted face-to-face by communities of practice in small local scenes.

The event brought together publications that are shaping the global conversation about technology: Colossus, Stripe Press, Asterisk, and Orbital Studies.

Ten groups gathered for watch parties around the world, on five continents, including in Tokyo, Lima, Wellington, and a special screening in the House of Commons in Ottawa.

One theme stood out above the rest: Knowledge is embedded in social relationships and passed face-to-face. Books and artifacts are useful storage mechanisms, but knowledge lies in people, and follows them where they go.

We are all in dialogue with the thinkers and builders who precede us. It is our responsibility to situate ourselves in their lineages and continue their work.
